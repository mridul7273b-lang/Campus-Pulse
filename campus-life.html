Campus Pulse & Enclave

Nestled at the base of Dighi Hills along Alandi Road, AIT Pune sprawls across a 30-acre verdant sanctuary. The campus integrates world-class academic blocks, advanced prototyping spaces, residential quarters, and extensive athletic facilities into an eco-certified living learning habitat.

World-Class Facilities

Central Digital Library

50,000+ technical volumes, IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect digital subscriptions, and 24x7 air-conditioned reading pavilions.

02

Sports Complex & OAT

Synthetic basketball and tennis courts, squash courts, multi-station gymnasium, and Open Air Amphitheatre for cultural showcases.

Residential Quarters

Separate secure residential hostel blocks for boys and girls with high-speed campus WiFi mesh and hygienic mess halls.

04

Green Energy Enclave

Recognized in National Green University Ranking 2026 with rooftop solar arrays, zero-waste processing, and rainwater catchment reservoirs.

04.2 STUDENT BRANCHES & INNOVATION ECOSYSTEM

Student Culture

TECH & CODING

Open Source & Coding Club

Hosts 24-hour hackathons, competitive programming sprints on Codeforces, and open-source contributions to Linux and AI repositories.

ROBOTICS & HARDWARE

Robotics Club (AIT)

Constructs autonomous rovers, combat bots, and AI drones competing annually at national challenges including Robocon and IIT Techfests.

ENTREPRENEURSHIP & E-CELL

AIT e-Cell

Cultivates founder mentalities through keynote speaker series, venture design sprints, and direct seed round linkages with Pune angel investors.

CULTURE & LITERATURE

Cultural & Literary Society

Organizes annual inter-collegiate cultural fest 'PACE', literary debating tournaments, theater productions, and the AIT E-Magazine.

MOTORSPORTS & SAE

Formula Student & BAJA Racing

Designs and fabricates open-wheel formula electric race cars and all-terrain buggies for national and international motorsports derbies.

PROFESSIONAL CHAPTERS

IEEE & ACM Student Branches

Organizes technical symposiums, research paper clinics, and international workshops in connection with IEEE Pune Section.

ATHLETICS & SPORTS

Sports & Fitness Guild

Maintains competitive teams in football, basketball, squash, cricket, and athletics, competing in inter-university championships.

COMMUNITY & SOCIAL OUTREACH

NSS & Social Responsibility Cell

Leads community blood drives, rural education outreach, eco-afforestation drives in Dighi Hills, and national disaster relief campaigns.

FAQ // 01 · AUTONOMOUS CURRICULUM

Which SPPU Autonomous electives yield highest lab scores?

Students entering 3rd year often struggle choosing between Cloud Native Architecture, Embedded IoT, and Advanced AI.

Senior Consensus: Cloud Native Architecture and Edge AI have 90%+ lab internal viva passing rates. Lab assignments are hands-on with Docker/K8s, directly aligning with Pune tech interviews.
FAQ // 02 · TECHNICAL INTERVIEWS

What is the baseline DSA milestone for 6th semester?

How many problems on LeetCode/CodeChef are needed to comfortably clear Day-1 technical coding assessments?

Senior Consensus: 150-180 problems on Striver's A2Z or NeetCode 150 (Graphs, Dynamic Programming, Trees, Heaps). More important than volume is explaining time/space complexity fluently in Java or C++.
FAQ // 03 · PROFESSOR PROJECT GUIDES

How to select final-year capstone project guides?

Which research areas get maximum patent/paper support from faculty in Computer and ARE divisions?

Senior Consensus: Submit project proposals early in 6th semester. Faculty who lead IIC (Institution's Innovation Council) provide lab space in Ramanujan Block and sponsor conference publishing fees.
FAQ // 04 · DIGHI HILLS LIFE HACKS

Hostel study hacks & curfew survival

Tips on managing late-night hackathon coding sessions, campus library access, and off-campus study spots.

Senior Consensus: Digital Library 2nd floor has 24/7 power during end-sem exam weeks. Always submit gate outpass on ERP 4 hours prior. For cab sharing to Pune railway station, post on the ON CAMPUS feed!
FAQ // 05 · OPEN SOURCE & HACKATHONS

How to break into GSOC & national hackathons?

When should 2nd and 3rd year students start contributing to public repositories and attending hackathons?

Senior Consensus: Attend OSS Club workshops in September. Start with documentation and good-first-issues in October. Join teams for Innerve in February — building end-to-end prototypes is what impresses recruiters.
FAQ // 06 · HIGHER STUDIES & GATE

Balancing placements with GATE & CAT prep

How seniors managed 7th semester company drives alongside national entrance exam preparation.

Senior Consensus: Secure a solid baseline campus placement by October. Once placed, transition your full focus to GATE/CAT mock tests in Central Library.

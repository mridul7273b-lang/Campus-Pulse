06 HERITAGE & INSTITUTIONAL VALUES

Founded on discipline, driven by pioneering technological curiosity.

Established in August 1994 by General B.C. Joshi, PVSM, AVSM, ADC, for the children of serving and retired Indian Army personnel, Army Institute of Technology (AIT) Pune has evolved into one of India’s foremost centers of engineering education.

From Academic Year 2025-26, AIT enters an era of academic autonomy under Savitribai Phule Pune University, enabling rapid curriculum innovation tailored to quantum computing, deep AI, autonomous robotics, and advanced semiconductor design.

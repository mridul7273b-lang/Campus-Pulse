RESEARCH DIVISIONS CENTRES OF EXCELLENCE & ADVANCED LABS

High-Throughput Laboratories

ADVANCED COMPUTING

High Performance Computing & AI Cluster

Multi-node NVIDIA Tensor Core GPU cluster supporting large language models, computer vision neural networks, and real-time medical imaging classification.

  • Architecture: Multi-GPU NVIDIA Tensor Core Nodes
  • Research Focus: Generative AI, Edge Inference & Defense Vision
  • Output: 45+ Scopus publications and funded research papers
INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION

PLC & Industrial Sensor Integration Lab

State-of-the-art facility featuring industrial Siemens and Allen-Bradley programmable logic controllers, analog sensor interaction benches, and SCADA consoles.

  • Hardware: Siemens S7-1200, Analog Sensor Racks, Smart Pneumatics
  • Application: Automated sorting, Industry 4.0 process telemetry
  • Funding: AICTE MODROBS & Institutional Research Grant
CYBER DEFENSE & NETWORKS

Cisco Networking & Cyber Range

Enterprise Gigabit routing racks, hardware firewalls, and isolated cyber-range sandboxes for penetration testing, zero-trust protocols, and network packet forensics.

  • Hardware: Managed Cisco Catalyst Switches & ASA Firewalls
  • Accreditation: Certified Cisco Networking Academy Partner
  • Focus: Cryptographic defenses, SDN architectures
SEMICONDUCTOR & VLSI

Advanced VLSI & Embedded Systems Studio

Semiconductor design workstation running licensed Cadence toolchains, Mentor Graphics EDA, and FPGA development kits for high-speed digital ASIC design.

  • Toolsets: Cadence Virtuoso, Xilinx Vivado, ARM Cortex-M0
  • Specialization: Low-power VLSI, RF transceivers, Edge SoCs
  • Degree Link: M.Tech VLSI & Embedded Systems Core Lab
